# Image Generation Prompt: Math Scribe Favicon (Pencil Edition)

## Overview
A clean, minimalist favicon for Math Scribe featuring a diagonal pencil alongside a simple mathematical equation "x + 4 = 7". This design immediately communicates the app's purpose: a tool for writing mathematics. The icon must be instantly recognizable at tiny sizes (16x16px) while maintaining a modern, accessible aesthetic.

**Target Audience**: Students, educators, accessibility advocates, and math professionals seeking a reliable mathematical transcription tool.

## Visual Style
- **Style**: Geometric, ultra-modern, minimalist with clean lines
- **Layout**: Diagonal pencil (top-left to bottom-right) with equation positioned nearby
- **Color Scheme**:
  - Primary: #3B82F6 (Rich Blue) - Pencil body
  - Accent: #F59E0B (Warm Orange/Amber) - Pencil tip and eraser
  - Text: #FFFFFF (White) or #F3F4F6 (Light Gray) - Equation text
  - Background: #1F2937 (Dark slate solid color) or fully transparent - **CRITICAL: NO checkered/checkerboard patterns**
- **Typography**: Clean sans-serif for equation (e.g., Inter, SF Pro, or similar modern typeface)
- **Icons**: Geometric simplicity - recognizable pencil shape, clear readable text

## Icon Design Specification

### Primary Element: Pencil (50% Visual Weight)
- **Form**: Simplified geometric pencil shape at 45° diagonal angle
- **Components**:
  - **Body**: Long rectangular form in rich blue (#3B82F6)
  - **Tip**: Small triangular point in warm orange (#F59E0B)
  - **Eraser**: Small rectangular cap at opposite end in warm orange (#F59E0B)
  - **Ferrule** (optional): Thin band between body and eraser (can be omitted for simplicity)
- **Position**: Diagonal from top-left toward bottom-right, or top-right toward bottom-left
- **Style**: Bold, clean lines with 6-10px stroke width
- **Details**: Minimal - just enough to be recognizable as a pencil
  - At 16×16px should read as diagonal line with pointed end
  - No wood grain, no shading, no pencil lead detail

### Secondary Element: Math Equation (50% Visual Weight)
- **Content**: "x + 4 = 7" (simple, recognizable equation)
- **Typography**:
  - Clean sans-serif font
  - Bold or semi-bold weight for visibility
  - Size: Large enough to remain readable at 16×16px (test critically!)
- **Color**: White (#FFFFFF) or light gray (#F3F4F6) for contrast
- **Position**: Options to test:
  1. **Below pencil**: Equation horizontal beneath diagonal pencil
  2. **Beside pencil**: Equation to the right or left of pencil
  3. **Integrated**: Equation appears to be "written" by the pencil tip
- **Style**: Clean, geometric, modern
- **Spacing**: Generous spacing between characters for legibility when small

### Technical Specifications
- **Base Resolution**: 512×512px (design and test at this size)
- **Minimum Viable Size**: Must remain crisp and readable at 16×16px
- **Stroke Weight**: 6-10px for pencil, bold font weight for equation
- **Aspect Ratio**: Perfect 1:1 square
- **File Format**: SVG preferred (scalable), PNG fallback at 512px, 256px, 64px, 32px, 16px
- **Transparency**: Support transparent background for flexible placement
- **Critical Test**: Scale to 16×16px - equation must still be readable

### Color Variants Required
1. **Primary Version**: Blue pencil + orange accents on dark/transparent background
2. **Light Mode**: Same colors on white background (ensure sufficient contrast)
3. **High Contrast**: Black pencil on white background, or white pencil on black

## Composition Guidelines
- **Whitespace**: Generous - don't crowd the 512px canvas
- **Padding**: Minimum 50px safe zone from edges (icon lives within central 412px)
- **Visual Balance**: Pencil and equation share equal visual importance
- **Alignment**: Consider visual alignment - equation baseline should feel deliberate
- **Simplicity**: Test at 16×16px frequently - ruthlessly remove any detail that muddies clarity
- **Emphasis**: Both elements must be recognizable - pencil as a writing tool, equation as mathematics
- **Layout Flow**: Natural reading flow (left-to-right, top-to-bottom)

## Mood and Brand Personality
- **Authoritative**: Clean geometric forms, mathematical precision
- **Approachable**: Friendly pencil symbol, simple equation (not intimidating)
- **Accessible**: High contrast, clear forms, works for color-blind users
- **Intelligent**: Mathematical equation signals computational capability
- **Confident**: "This is a tool that *works*" - no decoration, pure function
- **Modern**: Flat design, no gradients, contemporary 2026 design language
- **Educational**: Pencil + math immediately signals learning/academic context

## Design Variations to Test

### Pencil Orientation:
1. **Top-left to bottom-right**: Classic diagonal (/) orientation
2. **Top-right to bottom-left**: Reverse diagonal (\) orientation
3. **Steeper angle**: More vertical pencil for dynamic feel
4. **Shallow angle**: More horizontal for stability

### Equation Position:
1. **Below pencil**: Equation horizontally aligned beneath diagonal pencil
2. **Right of pencil**: Equation to the right, creating L-shape composition
3. **Integrated**: Pencil tip points to or "writes" the equation
4. **Above pencil**: Equation on top, pencil below (less conventional)

### Equation Variations:
1. **x + 4 = 7** (recommended - simple addition)
2. **2x = 6** (shows variables and coefficients)
3. **a² = 9** (includes superscript for visual interest)
4. **x = 3** (simplest possible - just variable and value)

### Background Options:
1. **Solid dark slate** (#1F2937): Professional, modern tech aesthetic - RECOMMENDED
2. **Fully transparent**: For flexible placement on any background (PNG with alpha channel)
3. **Solid dark blue** (#1E3A8A): Monochromatic, cohesive with pencil color
4. **Solid white** (#FFFFFF): For light mode contexts

**CRITICAL: NO checkered/checkerboard backgrounds. Background must be either a solid color OR fully transparent (no partial transparency patterns).**

## Special Considerations for Text in Icons

**Font selection is critical:**
- Use geometric sans-serif fonts (Inter, SF Pro, Roboto, Montserrat)
- Bold or semi-bold weight for maximum legibility
- Avoid decorative math fonts - clarity over authenticity
- Variable "x" should be clearly distinguishable from multiplication "×"

**Size testing for equation:**
- At 16×16px, the equation might need to be the ONLY visible element (pencil becomes context)
- Consider if equation alone could work as ultra-small fallback
- Test with anti-aliasing off - ensure pixel grid alignment

**Simplification strategy:**
- 512px: Full pencil detail + clear equation
- 256px: Simplified pencil + clear equation
- 64px: Basic pencil shape + readable equation
- 32px: Pencil silhouette + equation (both visible)
- 16px: May need to choose equation OR pencil as primary element
