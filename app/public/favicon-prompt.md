# Math Scribe Favicon Prompt

**Concept:** A hand writing a single equals sign. The equals sign is made of mathematical notation (a line with small variables or numerals floating around it).

**Style:** Geometric, sans-serif, ultra-modern. Bold strokes. No gradients—flat color with 2-3 accent colors max.

**Visual Hierarchy:** Hand is primary (70%), equation element is secondary (30%). They integrate seamlessly, not separate.

**Color:** Rich blue primary (#3B82F6 or equivalent), white space, one accent color (maybe a muted coral or teal for the equation). Dark background or transparent.

**Technical:** 512×512px base. Strokes should be 8-12px thick to remain crisp at 16×16. Perfect 1:1 ratio. Minimal details—every line must earn its place.

**Mood:** Authoritative yet approachable. Accessible, intelligent, confident. This is a tool that *works*.
